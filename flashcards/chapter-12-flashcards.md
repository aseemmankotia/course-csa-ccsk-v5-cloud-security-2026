## Chapter 12: Full CCSK v5 Exam Simulation and Time-Management Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | CCSK exam: total time and question count? | 90 minutes, 60 multiple-choice questions. Exam tip: budget ~1.5 minutes per question to finish with review time left over. |
| 2 | CCSK exam passing score? | 80%. Exam tip: you can miss up to 12 of 60 questions and still pass — don't let one hard question derail your pacing. |
| 3 | How much time should you reserve at the end for reviewing flagged questions? | 10–15 minutes. Exam tip: stop new questions with enough buffer to revisit flags — don't discover time is out mid-review. |
| 4 | Governance vs. Management — key exam distinction? | Governance = policies, oversight, accountability, board/risk-owner structures. Management = operational execution (patching, configuring controls). Exam tip: look for 'who decides/oversees' (governance) vs. 'who executes' (management). |
| 5 | CCM vs. CAIQ — what's the difference? | CCM = the control framework/catalog (17 domains). CAIQ = the standardized questionnaire a CSP fills out to self-assess against CCM controls. Exam tip: CCM is the 'what,' CAIQ is the 'how you ask a provider about it.' |
| 6 | RTO vs. RPO — key exam distinction? | RTO = max acceptable downtime (time to restore service). RPO = max acceptable data loss (measured in time). Exam tip: 'lose at most X minutes of data' = RPO; 'back online within Y hours' = RTO. |
| 7 | CSA Security Guidance vs. ENISA Cloud Computing Risk Assessment — when does the exam favor each? | CSA Security Guidance = 14-domain narrative best-practices reference, general cloud security guidance. ENISA guidance = risk-assessment focused, often cited for specific top cloud risks/vulnerabilities. Exam tip: if the question mentions 'risk assessment' terminology or EU context, ENISA is often the intended reference. |
| 8 | Qualifier words BEST / MOST / LEAST — why do they matter on CCSK scenario questions? | They determine which single answer is correct among several plausible options; missing 'LEAST' or 'MOST' inverts the intended answer. Exam tip: always re-read the final sentence of a scenario question before selecting. |
| 9 | CCSK question triage strategy — 3 steps | 1) Answer confidently within ~1.5 min if you know it. 2) If unsure, select best guess and flag. 3) Move on immediately — never get stuck. Exam tip: a flagged guess beats a blank answer if time runs out. |
| 10 | Final review window strategy — how to prioritize flagged questions? | Review flagged questions only, starting with the ones you're LEAST confident about. Exam tip: don't re-read every question — you won't have time, and it risks second-guessing correct answers. |
| 11 | Most efficient way to search open-book PDF references during the timed exam? | Use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F) with a specific keyword/control ID to jump directly to the relevant section instead of reading linearly. Exam tip: know the rough structure of CCM and CSA Guidance beforehand so your search terms are precise. |
| 12 | Cross-domain concept most commonly confused on the CCSK exam? | Governance vs. Management, closely followed by CCM vs. CAIQ and RTO vs. RPO. Exam tip: dedicate final review time specifically to these three pairs since they appear across multiple domains, not just one chapter. |
