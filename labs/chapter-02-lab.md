# Chapter 2 Lab: Domain 2: Cloud Governance and Domain 4: Organization Management

**Estimated time:** 18 minutes

## Exercise

Draft a two-part artifact: a governance policy hierarchy diagram (policy → standard → procedure) and an organization/tenant account structure with guardrails for a fictional multi-cloud company

## Key takeaway

Governance sets the policy direction; organization management implements it structurally through account hierarchy and enforced guardrails.
