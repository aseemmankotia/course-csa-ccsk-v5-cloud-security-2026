# Chapter 11 Lab: Domain 12: Related Technologies and Strategies

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Map a Zero Trust policy decision flow (subject, resource, context, policy engine, policy enforcement point) for a remote employee accessing a sensitive cloud application, then identify where SASE would enforce that policy at the network edge

## Key takeaway

Zero Trust and SASE provide the architectural foundation for modern cloud security, and AI/GenAI adoption introduces new data-leakage and supply-chain risks that these same principles must be extended to cover.
